Hi! I'm Cory

I’m a Minneapolis‑based graphic designer with a soft spot for weird, wonderful art and design. I love seeing how strange and inventive people can get with their work — the kind of creativity that bends the rules, stretches the edges, and makes you look twice. I’m always exploring new ways to express ideas, whether I’m drawing, sewing, experimenting with game design, or diving into whatever craft catches my curiosity next. Creativity is where I feel most at home, and I’m happiest when I’m making something that sparks imagination or brings a little unexpected beauty into the world.
